kidDo

What happens when computing
becomes personal for kids?

The personal computers of the past shaped a generation because they were understandable, hackable, and personal. kidDo reimagines those qualities for today's children and explores what happens when personal computing becomes personal for kids again.

Try it in your browser

Truly personal

kidDo is designed to belong to a child.

Not a classroom. Not a lab. Not a family's shared computer.

A child carries it, customizes it, fills it with games, music, drawings, messages, projects, and ideas. Over time it becomes part of their digital life, growing with them from their first creations to increasingly sophisticated projects.

Built for making

Children learn best when they are creating something that matters to them.

kidDo starts with creativity. Drawing sprites, composing music, building games, designing worlds, telling stories, and sharing projects all come before formal programming. Programming remains important, but as a tool for expanding what children can create, not as an end in itself.

Creations you can hold

Cartridges are more than storage.

They are games, projects, hardware extensions, inventions, and gifts. Children can exchange them with friends, modify them, build on each other's ideas, and create entirely new experiences.

Every cartridge is programmable. The goal is not to follow instructions but to discover possibilities: from making a game to controlling a robot, inventing a gadget, or building something nobody expected.
