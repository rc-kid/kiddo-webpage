I am particularly happy to share this news because, for me, it is really big.

The project that started almost four years ago as a pile of leftover electronics has, through countless late-night hours, evolved into something in which everything I ever cared about has converged. For quite a while now, I have known that this is no longer RCKid, if it ever really was. It has long surpassed the original idea of a remote controller for kids and become something much broader: a device for kids who do things.

The rename itself has felt inevitable for quite some time. While the name kidDo slowly started appearing throughout the project's code, documents, and experiments, I was waiting to make it official until I could offer it a proper home.

That time has finally come.

I have accepted a full-time position at the Czech Technical University, where kidDo, and the question of how technology can help children understand and act upon their world, will become my primary research focus.

This does not mean kidDo is finished. Quite the opposite, actually. There is still an enormous amount of work ahead. But it does mean I can finally remove "Designed after 11 PM" from the back of the device.

And I do hope that will have important implications for the project's future. :)
